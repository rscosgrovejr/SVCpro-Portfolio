# SVCpro

**Custom Service Management & Business Workflow Application**

## Application Screenshot

![SVCpro Dashboard](svcpro%20image.png)

SVCpro is a business application project focused on managing service operations, organizing customer information, and improving day-to-day business workflows.

## Project Objectives

- Centralize customer and service information
- Organize service requests and work activities
- Reduce repetitive administrative tasks
- Improve visibility into operational workflows
- Provide a foundation for business-specific customization and integrations

## Engineering Approach

SVCpro demonstrates my approach to custom business software development: understand how a business operates, model its information and processes, and build an application around its actual requirements.

My emphasis is on maintainable architecture, practical user interfaces, reliable data management, and software that can evolve as business needs change.

## My Role

- Business requirements analysis
- Application and database architecture
- Workflow and user-interface design
- Application development
- Integration planning
- Testing and deployment
